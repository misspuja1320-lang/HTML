FESTIVAL

A festival is an organized event or celebration that typically involves a series of activities, performances, and gatherings centered around a particular theme, culture, or occasion. Festivals are often marked by lively atmospheres, communal participation, and an emphasis on entertainment, tradition, or reflection. Festivals are important for building community, promoting cultural exchange, and offering a sense of joy and collective memory.
